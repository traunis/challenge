Utrecht · since 2019

SINCE 2019, Crumb & Co has been baking in a quiet corner of Utrecht. Every morning we bake sourdough, pastries and cakes, fresh. The sourdough starter is called Gerrit. He’s five years old and honestly does most of the work.
